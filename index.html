About Us

Building Trust
Since 2003

K L Facility is a professional facility management organisation led by experienced industry specialists. The company has evolved from a strong technical background in kitchen and equipment maintenance into a full‑scale integrated facility management provider.

Over the years we have delivered services to armed forces training centres, hospitals, corporate offices and residential developments. Operations currently span Mumbai, Vizag, Bangalore and multiple remote locations, with an expanding footprint across emerging cities.

Our core principle is simple: create clean, safe and efficient environments while strictly adhering to statutory requirements. A structured management team, from directors to site staff, works as one unit to protect client assets and enhance workplace experience.
